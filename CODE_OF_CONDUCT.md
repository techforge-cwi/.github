# Code of Conduct

TechForge is a student club, and everything here should be welcoming to someone who just wrote their first line of code.

## Expected

- Be patient and helpful, especially with beginners.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep content appropriate for a school setting, in code, comments, commit messages, and assets.
- Give credit for other people's work.

## Not okay

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual, violent, or hateful content.
- Posting anyone's personal information without their consent.
- Deliberately breaking things, spamming, or committing malicious code.

## Rules that apply here too

- The [CWI Student Code of Conduct and student handbook](https://cwi.edu/student-handbook)
- The TechForge Discord server rules and project guidelines you agreed to

## Reporting

Message any officer on Discord. Reports stay between you and the officers. Officers may remove content or take away GitHub and Discord access for anyone who breaks these rules.
