We learn AI by building things together: games, bots, tools, and whatever else members dream up. Most of us are just getting started, and that's the point. Every project here is set up so beginners can make a real contribution, whether that's code, art, writing, or testing.

## What we do

- **Meet every other Wednesday** at 11:30 AM in APIN 1402, or join on Zoom.
- **Build club projects** here on GitHub, one small pull request at a time.
- **Keep up with AI** through a weekly news roundup and discussion in our Discord.

## Join us

TechForge is open to every CWI student, no experience needed. Come to a meeting or ask any officer to get you into our Discord. From there:

1. Agree to the server rules in **#welcome-and-rules**.
2. Agree to the project guidelines in **#onboarding**.
3. Run **`/github`** in a project channel and sign in with GitHub. You'll get an invite to this organization.
